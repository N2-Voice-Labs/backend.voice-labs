# Dormitory, insurance and visa

International freshmen are guaranteed 100% dormitory placement if they apply during the designated period for the first semester. From the second semester, placement depends on admission results and dormitory criteria; schedules are on the dormitory website. Early move-out refunds remaining days with an additional penalty of 100,000 KRW.
Dormitory 2 (Next to Inha Technical College), Quadruple (4 people), public bathroom: KRW 1,340,000(during 1 semester), including operating expenses, fixtures deposit money and student council fees. One meal chosen from breakfast, lunch or dinner Mon~Fri is included. International students are allowed into quadruple rooms only in the first semester. Dormitory 2: 032-860-7274; http://dorm.inha.ac.kr.
From March 1, 2021, international students are covered by national health insurance. Private insurance is mandatory for the first 6 months before alien registration and national health insurance enrollment; National Health Insurance subscribers receive a refund after payment. Inquiries: 1577-1000 (foreign language speed dial 7), 033-811-2000 (English, Chinese, Vietnamese, Uzbek); private insurance: 032-860-7039, Weekdays 9:00 am to 6:00 pm.

Dormitory reference-date ambiguity: the Spring guide heading says as of Spring 2026 but its note says Spring semester of 2025. The Fall guide heading says as of Fall 2026 but its note says Fall semester of 2025. Both quote KRW 1,340,000(during 1 semester); this is not a confirmed 2027 price.
The dormitory webpage says the fee is different every semester and to inquire apply@inha.ac.kr; Dormitory 2 quadruple charges are “Further notice”. It says international students can only stay in Dormitory 2; the guides state quadruple rooms only in the first semester.

Visa
※ Students will receive the visa application process by email.
- Applicants abroad: Based on the admission documents, the Inha University International Cooperation and Support Team will
send a
certificate of admission by e-mail -> The South Korean Consulate in the applicant’s home country will require submission of
necessary documents for visa issuance. (i.e. certificate of bank balance and diploma for the highest level of education
completed, etc.)
- Applicants residing in Korea: Successful applicants residing in Korea must submit the required documents for a visa change
or extension -> Applicants will be notified by e-mail
※ Please call Inha University International Cooperation and Support Team for visa inquiries: 032-860-7037~8 and/or
Interservice@inha.ac.kr
※ Students transferring from another university in Korea to our university must leave the country and re-enter with a new visa
issued by our university. However, in some individual cases, it may be possible to change the visa status within Korea, so you
must check directly with the Immigration Office.

[Submission of Bank Balance Certificate for Final Admitted Students]
* Final admitted students must submit the original bank balance certificate valid for student visa issuance when applying for the
Certificate of Admission. The Certificate of Admission can only be issued after submission of the valid financial document.
* The submission deadline for the bank balance certificate is the same as the final tuition payment deadline.
* Submission is exempted for holders of F-series visas (Overseas Koreans, Permanent Residents, etc.) and holders of higher-level
visas who do not require a separate student visa issuance.
[1] The bank balance must be maintained until the date of admission (it may be reconfirmed when applying for a visa change).
[2] The certificate must have been issued within 30 days from the start date of the online application period.
[3] The funds required to prove financial capability must not be raised within Korea. If the funds were prepared in Korea, the
applicant must provide proof explaining the reason and details.
[4] If the applicant receives a scholarship from a university, embassy, or other institution, the financial proof documents may be
replaced with an official scholarship confirmation letter issued by the government, university president, dean, etc.
[5] If the bank balance certificate is issued in a native language other than English or Korean, a translated copy must also be
submitted.
[6] Only in unavoidable cases where parents are unavailable, bank accounts under siblings’ names may be accepted.
[7] Applicants changing status from this university’s Korean Language Institute student (D-4 visa holder) to undergraduate
admission may prove financial capability with KRW 10,000,000 only if they remain enrolled in the university’s Korean Language
Institute until the admission date.
[8] Basically, if you are changing your visa here in Korea, you must submit a bank statement under your own name. No one else's
name is allowed.
