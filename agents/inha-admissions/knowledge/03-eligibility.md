# Undergraduate eligibility

Applicants and both parents must be non-Korean and must have obtained foreign citizenship before the applicant entered high school. Dual citizens regarded as Korean citizens cannot apply. Special family or nationality circumstances require supporting documents, such as divorce, remarriage, single parent, death, missing person or loss of Korean nationality certificates.
Freshmen: high school graduates or expected graduates, or applicants who can prove education higher than high school.
Transfers must have graduated high school abroad or in Korea.
2nd year transfer: at a regular Korean or foreign university equivalent to a Korean 4-year university, completed at least 1 full academic year (2 semesters, excluding seasonal sessions) and at least 32 credits; alternatively graduates or expected graduates of a foreign 2(3)-year junior college, or legally recognized equivalent associate-degree education.
3rd year transfer: at a Korean 4-year university, completed at least 2 full academic years (4 semesters, excluding seasonal sessions), with at least 65 credits (including seasonal session credits); the guide also lists graduates or expected graduates of the 2nd or 3rd year at a Korean technical college. At an equivalent foreign university, completed at least 2 full academic years and at least half the credits required for graduation; the guide also lists graduates or expected graduates of the 2nd or 3rd year at a technical college abroad. Another listed route is recognized equivalent college-graduate academic background (for regular university having earned 80 credits or more).
2. About Transfers
※ Transfers
- Transfer students can apply for majors which are not related to their previous major.
- A maximum of 4 semesters will be recognized for transfer students who completed more than 4 semesters at their previous
universities (or technical colleges).
- Students transferring from another university in Korea to our university must leave the country and re-enter with a new visa issued by
our university. However, in some individual cases, it may be possible to change the visa status within Korea, so you must check directly
with the Immigration Office.
- For transfer students, "Completion" means that the student has obtained a certain number of credits required for completion
of each grade level specified by each university. Please check the completion status before starting the application process.
- Current Inha University students and students on leave of absence are not eligible to apply to the transfer admission (in the
exception of the graduates)
