# Undergraduate required documents

Freshmen submit 1 copy of each listed item:
- Application Form printed after online application, with passport size - 3.5cm × 4.5cm ID photos.
- Attended or Currently Enrolled Overseas Schools Information Form, printed after online application; Korean high school graduates/expected graduates exempt.
- Language proficiency evidence: TOPIK or TOPIK IBT, Korean regular university language center completion, King Sejong Institute certificate, or official TOEFL iBT / IELTS. Only valid scores at online application submission, or Korean language completion within the last 2 years, accepted.
- Letter of Agreement for Academic Background Check, printed after online application; Korean high school graduates/expected graduates exempt.
- High School Diploma or Certificate of Expectant Graduation and transcripts, 1 copy each, with full marks. Korean high school applicants submit graduation/expected graduation certificate and academic records, transcript.
- Nationality and family relationship evidence for applicant and parents, stating nationality, personal information and relationship. Non-Chinese: family register or birth certificate, divorce/death certificates if applicable, English translation by notarized translator. Chinese: family register covering all members and original relationship proof with English translation by notarized translators; individual family registers where applicable.
- Applicant and parents' passport copies; national ID copies if passports unavailable, followed by passport copies when issued.
Freshman foreign-school authentication:
Non-Chinese: Apostille for Apostille countries; Korean Embassy/Consulate consular confirmation for non-Apostille countries. Original confirmations required.
Chinese general high school: Huikao transcripts, online English certificate from China Higher Education Student Information; replaceable by Gaokao (高考) results report or Certificate of Non-Implementation of Huikao. Graduation certificate: online CHSI; transcript with Apostille.
Chinese vocational and other high schools: for 普通中专 / 职业高中 / 成人中专, online local-education-authority graduation certificate with Apostille (online authenticity required), or offline issuance with School Information Confirmation Letter: local-authority certificate with Apostille, or school-issued certificate with Seong Office of Education (City Office of Education) confirmation plus Apostille. 技工学校: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security online inquiry plus Korean consul in China; online authenticity required. Other recognized high schools: school-issued certificate plus Apostille and establishment permit copy (business unit law certificate or civil edition school license); only academic-background curriculum recognized, notarization required.
Transfers (2nd year/3rd year) submit 1 copy of each listed item:
- Application Form with passport size - 3.5cm × 4.5cm photos, printed after online application.
- Overseas Schools Information Form and prescribed Letter of Agreement for Oversea Academic Background Check, printed after online application; Korean university graduates/expected graduates exempt.
- Korean-track language documents: TOPIK or TOPIK IBT, Korean regular university language center completion, King Sejong Institute certificate; valid scores at online submission or completion within the last 2 years. English-track applicants or scholarship applicants: official TOEFL iBT / IELTS.
- High School Graduation Certificate; high school transcript is not required. If already graduated from previous university, only a certificate copy, without Apostille or embassy consular confirmation. Otherwise non-Chinese applicants use Apostille or Korean consular confirmation in the school's country; Chinese general high school applicants use online CHSI graduation certificate; Chinese vocational/other-school rules are as above. Original Apostille/consular confirmations required.
- University Diploma or Certificate of Expected Graduation and transcripts, 1 copy each, with full marks. Foreign universities outside China: Apostille, or Korean embassy/consulate confirmation in the school's country for non-Apostille countries. Chinese university diploma: Verification Report of China Higher Education Qualification Certificate (www.chsi.com.cn), Credentials Report (www.cdgdc.edu.cn); university transcripts: Apostille. Original Apostille/consular confirmations required.
- Nationality and family relationship evidence as for freshmen, but non-Chinese family documents may be translated into Korean or English by notarized translator. Chinese family relationship proof requires English translation by notarized translators.
- Applicant and parents' passport copies or national ID copies if unavailable; passport copies required when issued.
Performance evaluation materials for applicable freshman/transfer majors:
Kinesiology: Video. Design Convergence, Fine Arts, Fashion Design and Textiles, Design Technology: Portfolio, with optional list of accomplishments; approximately 5 items, free-form, art index and description. Theater and Film Studies: Portfolio and Video, art index and description. Physical Education: Portfolio, sports activities and tournaments participation/achievement. Explanatory material must be Korean or English.
General submission rules:
Submit documents in proper order. Documents not in English or Korean need English or Korean translations; the notice requires notarized translator translations. Different names require a same-person certificate from the applicant's home-country court. Korean high-school attendance periods require school life records instead of transcripts and enrollment proof. Foreign university completion requires proof of graduation credits, semesters completed and grade calculation standards. Apostille-country foreign school graduation certificates and transcripts need Apostille; non-member countries need Korean Consulate certification (the notice exempts universities approved by the Ministry of Education).
Expected graduates and transfer applicants submitting completion/expected graduation certificates must submit final graduation and academic-background documents after acceptance by the date set by the university. Documents are not returned; additional documents may be requested.
Submit by post or in person to the Global Talent Recruitment Team office: Postal code: 22212, Room 502, 5th floor, Kimhyuntae Inha Dream Center, Inha university, 100 inha-ro, Michuhol-gu, Incheon, South Korea.
Visit submission on weekdays 09:00 ~ 17:00 (Weekends & Holiday closed).
