# Undergraduate colleges and majors

Freshman Korean-track offerings by college:
- Engineering: Mechanical Engineering; Aerospace Engineering; Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering; Industrial Engineering; Department of Chemical and Energy Engineering (Chemical Engineering Major, Secondary Battery Engineering Major); Polymer Science and Engineering; Materials Science Engineering; Civil Engineering; Environmental Engineering; Geo-informational Engineering; Faculty of Architecture; Energy Resources Engineering; Electrical and Electronic Engineering; Semiconductor Systems Engineering.
- Bio Convergence: Biological Engineering; Biological Sciences; Advanced Biopharmaceuticals; Biofood Engineering.
- AI Convergence: Artificial Intelligence Engineering; Data Science; Smart Mobility; Design Technology; Computer Engineering.
- Natural Science: Mathematics; Statistics; Physics; Chemistry; Ocean Sciences; Food and Nutrition.
- Business Administration: Business Administration (Business Administration); Business Administration (Finance and Management); Asia Pacific School of Logistics; International Trade.
- Social Science: Public Administration; Political Science and International Relations; Media Communication; Economics; Consumer Science; Child Studies; Social Welfare Studies.
- Humanities: Korean Language and Literature; History; Philosophy; China Studies; Japanese Language and Culture; English and European Humanities Convergence; Cultural Contents and Management.
- Nursing: Nursing.
- Education: Physical Education (Recruiting 3 students).
- Arts and Sports: Fine Arts; Design Convergence; Kinesiology; Theater and Film Studies; Fashion Design and Textiles.
- International Studies: KLC(Korean Language & Culture); Division of Global Liberal Studies (choose major at 2nd year).
Freshman English-track offerings: IBT(International Business & Trade) and ISE(Integrated System Engineering), English Lectures 100%.
Spring table: Nursing and Physical Education recruit only in the spring semester; Architecture (5-year curriculum) only in Spring semester. Fall table instead says only Fall semester for all three. The Fall guide's architecture note nevertheless says the 5-year program may apply only for the March intake. These Architecture statements conflict; intake eligibility needs admissions confirmation.
Transfer offerings (2nd or 3rd year): Engineering includes Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Chemical Engineering Major, Polymer Science & Engineering, Materials Science Engineering, Civil Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Geo-informational Engineering, Architecture(Architectural Engineering), Energy Resources Engineering, Electrical and Electronic Engineering. Bio Convergence: Biological Engineering, Biological Sciences. AI Convergence: Computer Engineering. Natural Science, Business Administration, Social Science and Humanities list the majors above. Arts and Sports lists Fashion Design and Textiles only. International Studies lists IBT, ISE and KLC.
Transfer English tracks: IBT, ISE and GLOBA courses in Business Administration (Business Administration), Business Administration (Finance and Management), and Asia Pacific School of Logistics. GLOBA courses do not accept 2nd year transfers; the Business Administration (Business Administration) GLOBA course is only open to students pre-approved through consultation with the partner university or department. General courses are Korean Track.
Arts and Physical Education departments and Design Technology require performance evaluation materials; see the required documents file. University names, departments and quotas may change following internal deliberations and Ministry of Education approval.
