No additional application rounds are offered.
The number of admission rounds and application periods are subject to change.
For admission information regarding other graduate schools at Inha University, please visit the respective graduate school's website and contact its administrative office.

Tuition Refund
Students who wish to cancel their enrollment may do so by submitting the Application for Enrollment Cancellation and Tuition Refund along with the required supporting documents.
If the cancellation request is submitted before the first day of the semester, the admission fee and tuition fee will be fully refunded.
If a refund request arises after the first day of the semester, the student will be subject to the University's withdrawal procedures. For further information, please contact the Graduate School Administration Office.

Scholarships: Jeongseok International Scholarship is automatically assessed from submitted official language certificates. Types A, B, C and D cover 100%, 70%, 50% and 30% of Admission and Tuition Fees respectively, subject to their language criteria. Global Vision Scholarship covers 100% of Admission and Tuition Fees subject to recommendation and selection. See 09-scholarships.md for exact eligibility, duration, GPA and assistantship requirements.
