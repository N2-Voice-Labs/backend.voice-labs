# Application procedure and application fee

Selection: Document evaluation 100%, considering academic achievement, extracurricular activity and willingness to develop. High home-country university entrance examination scores receive priority consideration. Inconsistent documents can cause denial even after entry.
Online procedure: visit https://internationalcenter.inha.ac.kr and select the Spring 2027 or Fall 2027 International students admission application, or use www.studyinkorea.go.kr Online Application. Sign up and log in; select Inha University; read the guide; fill and save the form; pay by credit card, wire transfer, etc.; print the Application Form; submit documents by post or in person to Global Talent Recruitment Team.
Closing times use Korea Standard Time[KST]. No modifications after fee payment; check carefully before submitting. Contact changes: apply@inha.ac.kr / 82-32-860-8609. Online problems: Uway apply www.uwayapply.com, 1588-8988; Study in Korea problems: 032-860-8609.
Application Fee: KRW 150,000 (paid online after submitting online application). Application is final only after online requirements and fee payment are completed.
Application fee refunds are unavailable after online completion except: overpayment (overpaid amount); inability to complete due to university issues (full amount); natural disasters (full amount); hospitalization due to disease/accident or applicant's death (full amount, supporting documents required).
An admission income/expenditure balance is returned proportionally under the cited Higher Education Act provisions. Request wire transfer during online application period or visit Admission Office. Transfer charges are deducted; no refund if charges exceed the refund amount.
