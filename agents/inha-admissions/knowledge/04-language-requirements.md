# Undergraduate language requirements

Freshmen, Korean Track: TOPIK or TOPIK IBT Level 3 or higher, Level 4 or higher from a Korean language program at a university in Korea, or King Sejong institute intermediate-4A course completed or higher. Admission department recognition of equivalent language ability is also listed.
Division of Global Liberal Studies: TOPIK Level 2 or completion of Level 3 Korean language course at a Korean university language institute permits entry with intensive first-year Korean education and a separate fee (approximately KRW 1.5 million per year). Entry to the desired major in the second year requires completion of first-year curriculum, designated Korean courses, and TOPIK Level 3 or higher; otherwise College of International Studies regulations apply. Applicants with TOPIK Level 3 or above are exempt from additional Korean education and may enter their desired major in the second year.
Transfers, Korean Track: TOPIK or TOPIK IBT Level 4 or higher, Level 5 or higher at a Korean university language program, or King Sejong institute intermediate-4A completed or higher; admission department recognition of equivalent ability is also listed. The guide calls the exception departments “Department of Child Psychology” and “Department of Cultural Contents and Cultural Management”: TOPIK or TOPIK IBT level 3 or higher, domestic university Korean course level 4 or higher, or King Sejong Institute Intermediate-4A or higher.
English Track: freshmen IBT, ISE; transfers IBT, ISE, GLOBA. IELTS 5.5 or above, or TOEFL iBT 71 or TOEFL iBT 4(for applicants who took the test after January 21,2026) or above. TOEFL iBT “MyBest Scores” accepted if each section is valid within the last 2 years; IELTS “One Skill Retake” accepted. TOEFL iBT Home Edition and IELTS Indicator are not accepted. Students from countries where English is their first language or the country's official language need not submit language proficiency documents. Admission department recognition of equivalent ability is also listed.
Scores must be valid at online application submission; Korean program completion certificates must be within the last 2 years.
Graduation requires TOPIK level 4 or higher and departmental graduation regulations including the stipulated English proficiency test. English-track students follow departmental regulations; GKS and foreign-government scholarship students can be individually screened at the department.
