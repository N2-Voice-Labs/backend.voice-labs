# Registration, cancellation and refunds

Undergraduate cancellation: submit to the Global Talent Recruitment Team Office 1 copy each of Tuition Refund Request Form (prescribed form), Tuition Payment Receipt, applicant's ID and applicant's bank book. The undergraduate tuition-refund section does not state a dated percentage refund schedule.
Not registering within the registration period is treated as waiver and admission is canceled. Double enrollment at two or more universities in the same semester causes cancellation. Visa issuance refusal causes cancellation; failure to obtain a visa within two weeks after semester commencement without special reason also causes cancellation. The student is responsible for failure to receive a visa.
Forgery, alteration or lack of application qualification causes cancellation with no tuition refund. Incorrect online information, missing essential documents, written errors, omissions and illegibility are the applicant's responsibility. Results/evaluation details are not disclosed; final acceptance must be checked on the international admissions website, with no individual notice.
Division of Global Liberal Studies Korean language tuition paid on TOPIK Level 2 entry is non-refundable even if TOPIK Level 3 is obtained during enrollment.
Graduate cancellation: submit Application for Enrollment Cancellation and Tuition Refund and supporting documents. Before the first day of the semester, admission fee and tuition fee are fully refunded. After the first day, university withdrawal procedures apply; contact Graduate School Administration Office.
